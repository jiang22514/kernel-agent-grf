The unranked catalogue contains all 94 legal kernels. A candidate is one kernel

SE, MA1, MA3, MA5, and RQ are products of one-dimensional factors, with separate

period. Sums superpose independent components; products modulate components.
The catalogue's exclusions are search-space restrictions, not equivalences.

SEARCH APPROACH
* Start with four informative, contrasting hypotheses supported by the data.
  Spatial summaries are clues: a flat pooled variogram or weak one-dimensional
  spectrum does not rule out directional or interacting periodic structure.
  Strong OLS trend does not uniquely determine the GP mean or covariance.
* Keep several competing structures alive. Track up to three distinct kernel
  expressions, not three means of the same expression. Close observed AICs
  (for example, within 10 units) warrant follow-up rather than hard elimination.
  A second-best simple model may yield the best composite extension.
* Use feedback for informative comparisons: hold the mean fixed while changing
  a primitive or composition, and hold the kernel fixed while changing means.
  When two component choices remain plausible, compare matching extensions of
  both. Adding a component can change which roughness or modulation is useful;
  a component's poor standalone or pairwise result does not exclude synergy.
* Balance refinement with a credible alternative. Do not spend every later
  slot on descendants of the current first-ranked kernel. Revisit an untested
  structural explanation when its ingredients have not had a fair comparison.
  The supplied neighbors are suggestions, not a restriction on the catalogue.
* Before the final round, examine whether a promising kernel's mean has really
  been checked. Use the final four slots to resolve the most consequential
  remaining structure/mean comparisons. Mean alternatives each cost one full
  evaluation; no choice has a known score until requested.
* Allocate the four slots yourself based on observed evidence; avoid rigid
  family quotas. Give four primary distinct unvisited pairs plus two reserves.
  Only the first four valid unvisited pairs are evaluated. No invisible scoring,
  automatic mean optimization, or extra candidate evaluations are available.
